How to Download Pinterest Videos

Downloading a Pinterest video is straightforward when you have the video's share URL. A typical browser workflow is to copy the Pinterest link, open a compatible downloader, paste the URL, and save the available video file.

1. Copy the Pinterest video link

Open the Pinterest Pin containing the video and use Pinterest's share or copy-link option to copy its URL.

2. Open PinVideo.io

Go to PinVideo.io, the main browser-based Pinterest video downloading tool.

3. Paste the URL

Paste the copied Pinterest URL into the downloader field and follow the instructions shown by the service.

4. Save the video

If a downloadable result is available, save the file to your device. Availability can depend on the source Pin and the current behavior of Pinterest.

Responsible downloading

Download and reuse content only when you have the right to do so. Copyright and other rights can apply to Pinterest content even when a video is publicly visible.

Pinterest Video URLs: How to Find and Use Them

A Pinterest video URL is the web address associated with a Pin. When a Pin contains a video, the share/copy-link feature can provide the address you need for a browser-based downloader.

Finding a video URL

  1. Open the Pinterest Pin containing the video.
  2. Choose the share option.
  3. Select the option to copy the link.
  4. Paste the copied URL into your chosen browser-based tool.

Using the URL with PinVideo.io

PinVideo.io is designed to accept Pinterest links through a simple browser interface. Paste the Pin URL and follow the instructions displayed on the site.

If the link does not work

Check that you copied the complete URL, try opening the Pin directly in a browser, and make sure the Pin is still publicly accessible. Changes to Pinterest pages or individual Pins can affect whether a URL can be processed.

Rights and permissions

A URL does not grant ownership or permission to reuse a video. Follow the creator's rights and applicable platform terms before publishing or redistributing downloaded material.

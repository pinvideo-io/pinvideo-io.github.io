  <h2>What If a Pinterest Video Will Not Download?</h2>

  <p>
    Not every Pin will work the same way. If a video does not download, the problem does not
    necessarily mean that something is wrong with your device.
  </p>

  <h3>Check the URL</h3>

  <p>
    Start by making sure you copied the actual Pin URL. A profile, board, or search URL is different
    from the link to an individual Pin.
  </p>

  <h3>Try copying the link again</h3>

  <p>
    Sometimes the easiest fix is to return to Pinterest, open the Pin again, copy its link, and
    paste the new URL into the downloader.
  </p>

  <h3>The Pin may not contain an accessible video</h3>

  <p>
    A Pinterest page can contain different types of media. Some Pins may not expose a downloadable
    video in a way that an online service can process.
  </p>

  <h3>The content may be restricted</h3>

  <p>
    Private, restricted, removed, or otherwise inaccessible content may not be available for
    processing. An online downloader should not be expected to bypass those restrictions.
  </p>

  <h3>Pinterest may have changed something</h3>

  <p>
    Websites change their systems from time to time. When that happens, third-party tools may
    temporarily stop recognizing certain links until their systems are updated.
  </p>

  <h3>Try another browser</h3>

  <p>
    If the page itself is not behaving correctly, try a current version of another browser. Also
    check whether a browser extension is interfering with the download.
  </p>

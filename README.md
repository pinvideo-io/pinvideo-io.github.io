What If a Pinterest Video Will Not Download?
Not every Pin will work the same way. If a video does not download, the problem does not necessarily mean that something is wrong with your device.

Check the URL
Start by making sure you copied the actual Pin URL. A profile, board, or search URL is different from the link to an individual Pin.

Try copying the link again
Sometimes the easiest fix is to return to Pinterest, open the Pin again, copy its link, and paste the new URL into the downloader.

The Pin may not contain an accessible video
A Pinterest page can contain different types of media. Some Pins may not expose a downloadable video in a way that an online service can process.

The content may be restricted
Private, restricted, removed, or otherwise inaccessible content may not be available for processing. An online downloader should not be expected to bypass those restrictions.

Pinterest may have changed something
Websites change their systems from time to time. When that happens, third-party tools may temporarily stop recognizing certain links until their systems are updated.

Try another browser
If the page itself is not behaving correctly, try a current version of another browser. Also check whether a browser extension is interfering with the download.

Is Downloading Pinterest Videos Legal?
Downloading a video and having the right to use that video are two different things.

A video being publicly visible on Pinterest does not automatically mean that it is free to copy, publish, sell, or use in your own content. Videos may belong to their creators or other copyright holders.

The safest approach is to download content that you created yourself, content that you have permission to use, or content that is clearly offered for reuse under applicable terms or licenses.

If you plan to use a downloaded video commercially, repost it, edit it into another project, or publish it somewhere else, check the creator's permissions and the rules that apply to the content first.
